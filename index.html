02 / Systems Anatomy

Clinical rigor meets systems engineering.

Why dual-track discipline in medicine and computer science produces sharper software architecture.

Almost my entire technical foundation was learned, coded, and deployed from Android + Termux before moving to dedicated Linux environments. That constraint eliminated the luxury of bloated dependencies and sloppy tooling.

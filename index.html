01 / About

Medicine taught me to study systems. Engineering taught me to build them.

I’m currently a first-year MBChB student at the University of Nairobi while seriously exploring a future in computer science, especially systems, AI and security. I’m applying to NYU Abu Dhabi for Early Decision and MIT for Regular Decision in computer science.

That dual-track is not a contradiction. Medicine trains me to reason through complex, high-stakes systems; engineering gives me a way to design, automate and improve them. My projects live in the overlap: data, interfaces, infrastructure, automation and decision-support tools that turn messy workflows into usable systems.

Most of that journey happened under a practical constraint: Android + Termux instead of a laptop. That forced me to care about resource efficiency, reproducibility, lightweight tooling and infrastructure that works outside ideal conditions.
